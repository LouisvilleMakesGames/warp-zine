# FAQ for Submitting to WARP ZINE


## Who can submit content to WARP ZINE?
Anyone can submit content to WARP ZINE if you are in our geographical area or on the KY gamedev discord


## What type of content is accepted for submission?
Articles
* Games
* Tutorials
* Cover art


## Does a game have to be completed to be submitted?
No, games can be:
* Works-in-progress (WIP)
* Upcoming releases
* Already released


## What are the requirements for the front and back cover?
The cover art should be a single image that is 11x8.5 inches, folded in half


## What are the requirements for the centerfold?
The centerfold should be a single 8.5"x11" sheet
It can be a game sleeve or a large image


## What are the dimensions for a single page submission?
5.5" x 8.5" with a 0.55 margin


## What format should submissions be in?
Submissions can be in text, image file, or PDF format
If you have a designed page, you can submit it as an image file in the exact dimensions of the page


## How often are issues released?
Issues are released once a quarter


## What is the deadline for submissions?
Submissions are due a week before print, with the next issue's submissions due on March 25, 2025


## How will submissions be selected?
Submissions will be selected based on a variety of factors, including the quality of the content and the timeliness of the submission
Early submissions are encouraged, as they will be given priority consideration


## How do I submit my content?
Share links to your content in the #warpzine channel on discord and make sure someone on the crew notices


## What happens after I submit my content?
You might be asked to do revisions, or the WARPZINE crew might make slight changes to your layout to improve readability or printability of your pages, they will let you know if that happens


## Is the WARPZINE free?
Yes, 100% free in both print and digital form on [itch.io](https://louisvillemakesgames.itch.io/warp-zine-0)


## Do contributors get paid?
No, contributors are not paid, and the WARPZINE does not make any income for Louisville Makes Games


## Who runs the WARP ZINE?
Louisville Makes Games, a 501(c)3 non-profit, is the sponsor of the zine, but all content is community-generated, so opinions are strictly those of the individual creators and not endorsed by LMG. LMG encourages community members to stand up for social issues that affect marginalized voices in our local community, the broader gamdev community, and the world, as outlined in our [Code of Conduct](https://louisvillemakesgames.org/code-of-conduct.html)


## Where can I find examples of what works and what doesn't?
Issue 0 of WARP ZINE is available on [itch.io](https://louisvillemakesgames.itch.io/warp-zine-0), and can be used as a reference for what types of content and formatting work well in the zine.
